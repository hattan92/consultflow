Route Consultation

Medical Administration On-Call can hand the case to any rota level for the same specialty.
Routing rules: this action keeps the same consultation and Patient Journey, records Medical Administration On-Call as the routing user, preserves prior delay/escalation history, resets the active acceptance stage for the newly assigned person, and sends them a new alert. After they press Accept Consultation, their 30-minute attendance clock starts.

Add Employee

Save employee details once, then reuse them in weekly rota dropdowns.
Saved once, reused everywhere: name, employee ID, specialty, rank, mobile and WhatsApp are stored in the employee directory and automatically appear in rota scheduling and current on-call routing.

Weekly On-Call Rota

Sunday–Saturday. Daily assignments automatically become the effective rota for that date.
No repeated typing: every assignment is selected from the saved employee directory. Change an employee's phone/WhatsApp once in the directory and the rota continues to point to that same employee profile.

Manual Escalation

ED may manually escalate to a higher rota level when clinically or operationally required.
Automatic escalation remains active independently: after a physician accepts the consultation, the 30-minute attendance clock runs. If the threshold is exceeded and ED confirms care impact, the final 3-minute re-contact occurs; after the final ED confirmation, the system escalates automatically to the next rota level.

Manual escalation: ED can escalate earlier to any higher level. The action, reason, user, timestamp, previous level, and new level are audit logged. Any existing physician delay is preserved in KPI history.

Close & Redirect Consultation

ED-only workflow before physical specialty attendance.
New consultation: starts at First On-Call of the destination specialty and follows the same escalation pathway: First → Second → Third → Head of Department → Medical Administration On-Call.

Timers: referral-to-accept timing starts immediately for the new consultation. After Accept Consultation, the 30-minute physical-attendance clock starts; the agreed 3-minute final re-contact workflow applies if ED decides the delay affected patient care.

Copies / alerts: original specialty HOD, destination specialty HOD, Medical Administration On-Call, Medical Administration, and CQI & Patient Safety. Destination First On-Call receives the new consultation.
Important: If ED does not agree that another specialty should take the case, do not redirect. Use the Safety Admission pathway instead.
