Close & Redirect Consultation

ED-only workflow before physical specialty attendance.
New consultation: starts at First On-Call of the destination specialty and follows the same escalation pathway: First → Second → Third → Head of Department → Medical Administration On-Call.

Timers: referral-to-accept timing starts immediately for the new consultation. After Accept Consultation, the 30-minute physical-attendance clock starts; the agreed 3-minute final re-contact workflow applies if ED decides the delay affected patient care.

Copies / alerts: original specialty HOD, destination specialty HOD, Medical Administration On-Call, Medical Administration, and CQI & Patient Safety. Destination First On-Call receives the new consultation.
Important: If ED does not agree that another specialty should take the case, do not redirect. Use the Safety Admission pathway instead.
